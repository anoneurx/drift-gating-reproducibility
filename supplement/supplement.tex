\section{Local Protocol Record (Post Hoc Status)}
\label{sec:protocol}
The study hypotheses (H1--H3), endpoints, smallest worthwhile gain ($\ge 0.02$ net miss-ratio reduction on $\ge 2/3$ of traces in a family--capacity group), capacity levels ($1\%$, $5\%$, $10\%$ WSS), and controller configurations were recorded in the project git repository in \path{PATH_A_STUDY_PROTOCOL.md} (commit \path{ab5841c}) prior to executing \path{run_study.py}. \section{Zero-Cost Ceiling and Window Sensitivity}
\label{sec:zerocost}
\label{sec:window}
At $W=100{,}000$ references ($264$ pairs), the DP oracle at $c_{\text{switch}}=C$ yields mean net gain $0.0001$, median $0.0000$, maximum $0.0067$, and $0/264 \ge 0.02$. At $c_{\text{switch}}=0$ ($W=100{,}000$), mean net gain is $0.0007$, median $0.0000$, maximum $0.0419$, and $1/264$ clears $0.02$ (\path{fiu_online.cs.fiu.edu_test} at $10\%$ WSS).

\section{Reproducibility Package}
To regenerate all study CSVs, tables, figures, and PDFs from scratch:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compile simulator: \path{g++ -O3 -std=c++17 reproduction/pathA_study/pathA_sim.cpp -o reproduction/pathA_study/pathA_sim}
\item Run simulations: \path{python3 reproduction/pathA_study/run_study.py --workers 6}
\item Extract features: \path{python3 reproduction/pathA_study/extract_features.py}
\item Analyze \& generate LaTeX assets: \path{python3 reproduction/pathA_study/analyze_pathA_study.py} and \path{python3 reproduction/pathA_study/generate_paper_assets.py}
\item Build PDFs: \path{cd drift_gating_latex_source && python3 build.py && pdflatex supplement.tex && pdflatex supplement.tex}
\end{enumerate}
