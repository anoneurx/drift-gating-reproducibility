                                                                                    C. Metrics and Statistical Analysis
                                                                                       The primary metric is the miss ratio after warm-up, reported
                                                                                    with the percentage reduction relative to LRU. Detection is
                                                                                    evaluated on all 80 non-stable traces across four scenarios
                                                                                    (abrupt, gradual, recurring and composite; 380 true boundaries
                                                                                    in total, which is what the 278/380 figure denominates): a
                                                                                    boundary is detected if a confirmed alarm occurs within 30,000
                                                                                    references after it, and the delay is the distance to that alarm.
                                                                                    A confirmed alarm outside every such window is counted
                                                                                    as spurious. On stable traces confirmations are counted both
                                                                                    over the whole trace and after reference 100,000, the latter
                                                                                    excluding an initial transient. A reversal is a switch A→B→A
                                                                                    within 60,000 references. Cost is the simulated wall-clock
                                                                                    time per reference of the single released simulator on this
                                                                                    machine; it is indicative, not a kernel measurement. Recovery
                                                                                    time, memory overhead and full ranking agreement were not
                                                                                    measured in this reproducible study.
